Run:

2. Allow Location when Chrome asks.

5. The route, distance and ETA appear on the map.

Note:
The public OpenStreetMap/Nominatim/Photon/OSRM services are suitable for development/demo use, but production deployment should use a properly provisioned geocoding/routing provider with appropriate usage limits.
